# Project Master Plan — Yakubu Lado Dan Marke Campaign Command Platform

## Mission & Scope
Enterprise-grade Political Campaign Operations, Election Situation Room, Field Intelligence, and Command Center Platform covering all 36 States of Nigeria, the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), and all 774 verified Local Government Areas (LGAs).

## Core Principles
1. **No Fabricated Political or Electoral Facts**: Official party affiliation, slogan, campaign logos, and candidate portraits remain configurable placeholders until authorized campaign assets are uploaded via Settings & Administration or the Setup Wizard.
2. **Verified Geographic Backbone**: Built on Nigeria's verified 36 States + FCT (37 jurisdictions) and 774 authentic LGAs, with an extensible CSV/JSON/GeoJSON Import Wizard for official ward and polling unit datasets.
3. **Strict Data Integrity & Attribution**: Demo records are explicitly marked `DEMO DATA`, campaign reports are labeled `Campaign-Reported Data` (never official INEC results), and incidents strictly distinguish `Unverified Report`, `Allegation`, and `Verified Fact`.
4. **Ethical & Data Protection Boundaries**: Zero individual voter profiling, persuasion scoring, or covert surveillance. All analytics measure operational readiness, coverage, and response speed.

## 19-Module Delivery Roadmap (100% Delivered)
1. Executive Situation Room (live computed KPIs, Nigeria tactical GIS map, Wall Display Mode)
2. Campaign Overview
3. Geographic Operations (36 States + FCT + 774 LGAs + Geo Import Wizard & rollback)
4. Field Operations (Check-ins with opt-in GPS disclosure, Field Reports, Mobile Agent PWA Mode, Offline Sync Queue)
5. Agent & Volunteer Management
6. Call Center & Public Contact Desk (Operator Console & Supervisor Dashboard)
7. Incident Management (7-stage workflow, timeline audit, fact/allegation separation)
8. Campaign Events & Schedule (events, budgets, interactive checklists)
9. Logistics & Transportation (requests/approvals, fleet assets, inventory stock controls)
10. Campaign Organization (6-tier national-to-polling-unit hierarchy + departments)
11. Polling Unit Coverage (neutral coverage gap tracking + agent assignment)
12. Election-Day Operations (admin mode toggle, report verification pipeline, non-INEC disclaimer)
13. Reports & Analytics (Recharts visualizations, 10 executive templates, CSV/JSON/Print export)
14. Communications & Announcements (scoped broadcasts + consent compliance)
15. Public Media Monitoring (authorized public sources + classification review)
16. Campaign Document Vault (versioned SOPs + role-based access labels)
17. Tasks & Operational Workflow (List & Board views, 8 statuses, subtasks, reusable templates)
18. Security, RBAC & Audit Governance (21-role matrix + immutable audit explorer)
19. Settings, Branding Studio & Administration (live branding, alert rules, demo data reset, Setup Wizard)
